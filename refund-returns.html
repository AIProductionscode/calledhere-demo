REFUND & RETURNS POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

1. 30-Day Return Window 2. Item Eligibility 3. How to Return 4. Refund Processing 5. Exchanges 6. Damaged or Defective Items

1. 30-Day Return Window

We want you to love what you wear. If you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, you may return eligible items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund to your original payment method or store credit.

2. Item Eligibility

To qualify for a refund or exchange:

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and odor-free.
  • Original product tags and labels must remain attached.
  • Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.

3. How to Return

To start a return:

  1. Email our customer service team at support@calledhere.com with your Order ID (#CH-XXXXX) and the item(s) you wish to return.
  2. We will email you a prepaid return shipping label and packing slip.
  3. Securely pack your items and drop off the package with USPS.

4. Refund Processing

Once your return package is delivered to our fulfillment hub in Fishers, Indiana, our team will inspect the item within 3–5 business days. Your refund will be issued to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or credit card issuer, funds typically reflect in 5–10 business days.

5. Exchanges

Need a different size in your tee, hoodie, or sweatshirt? We are pleased to provide complimentary exchange shipping on domestic orders so you get the perfect fit.

6. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a flawed garment or print error, please send a photo along with your order ID to support@calledhere.com within 7 days of delivery. We will immediately replace your item at zero additional cost.
