PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview 2. Information We Collect 3. How We Use Information 4. Information Sharing 5. Cookies & Tracking 6. Data Security 7. Your Rights & Choices 8. Contact Us

1. Overview

Called Here Apparel ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes the practices we follow when you visit our website calledhere.com, purchase our apparel, or engage with our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies or relates to you, including:

  • Contact Information: Name, billing and shipping addresses, email address, and phone number.
  • Payment Information: Tokenized payment details processed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways. We do not store raw card numbers.
  • Order History: Details regarding apparel items purchased, sizes, and shipping dates.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, referral URLs, and pages visited.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Process, print, and deliver your orders accurately.
  • Send transaction receipts, delivery status SMS, and tracking information.
  • Communicate new collection launches and verse-of-the-week reflections (with your consent).
  • Improve site performance and safeguard our storefront from fraud.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share necessary details only with vetted service partners essential to completing your order (e.g. USPS/UPS delivery carriers and payment gateways).

5. Cookies & Tracking

We utilize first-party cookies necessary for core shopping cart functionality, retaining your bag items, and session security. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time.

6. Data Security

All data transmitted between your browser and our store is protected by industry standard 256-bit SSL encryption. We enforce administrative and technological safeguards to prevent unauthorized access.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your account. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with one click via the link in any newsletter.
